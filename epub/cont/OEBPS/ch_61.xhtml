1060 Episode 66. The Wolf That Swallowed a God (8)

As if mocking Odin's declaration, Yoo Joonghyuk of the 1,865th regression swung the 'Black Heavenly Demon Sword'. The moment the regressor's sword, perfected through endless training, touched the god's neck, Odin's single eye flared with light.

「He is the one who killed the primordial giant.」

The space around us warped, and Yoo Joonghyuk's slash vanished somewhere. I was startled by that astonishing power.

「Together with his brothers he claimed the first void and opened a myth with the corpse of that giant.」

Others might not know, but I alone knew what that power was.

[The Giant Story 'Ginnungagap' has begun its storytelling.]

Ginnungagap. The primordial power that <Asgard> had once used to deal with me and the 'Demon King of Salvation'. That powerful force was now being manifested by Odin alone.

Yoo Joonghyuk paid no mind to the disappearing slash and continued pouring out attacks.

[It is useless. O 'Outer God who searches for the Oldest Dream'!]

Every time a slash flew in, the void space of 'Ginnungagap' opened and closed repeatedly.

A power that nullified every single terrifying slash of the Yoo Joonghyuk of the 1,865th regression.

I knew Odin was a strong constellation. I had experienced it in the 40th regression, read about it in the main story, and even witnessed his battle against the Great Sage Heaven's Equal with my own eyes.

But the moment I looked at the Odin before me, I felt that I still did not truly know him.

「Only he would understand both the beginning and the end of a single myth.」

He was the existence who had killed Ymir, the master of <Asgard>'s Primordial Void (太虛) — 'Ginnungagap' — and become a god himself.

An unusual and ominous aura flickered in both of his hands as he wielded the void dimension of 'Ginnungagap'.

Black and white stories resided in Odin's hands. The right hand resembled pitch black darkness, and the left hand resembled pure white light. Those two hands that grasped the void were precisely the power that differentiated him from other 'Myth-grade Constellations'.

「Odin's right hand — the 'Primordial Will', Vili.」

I sensed the immense power of the stories dwelling in those two hands.

「Odin's left hand — the 'Divider of Order', Vé.」

The power of those two stars was also the source that had placed Odin upon the throne of <Asgard>.

「He devoured his own brothers and claimed the title of Master of the Primordial Void.」

The Trinity of Will, Order, and Wisdom.

Faced with Odin's might, who alone exercised a status equivalent to the combined power of three 'Myth-grade Constellations', I forgot even to breathe as I became absorbed in the battlefield.

Goosebumps continuously rose along my spine. There were two reasons. The first was pure astonishment at Odin's status, and the second was—

"Even a monster that powerful fled from 'Snow White', huh."

Hearing Yoo Joonghyuk of the 41st regression's voice, I nodded.

The enemy we would have to fight from now on was a monster that made even that Myth-grade Constellation tremble in fear.

And another monster who did not fear such an Odin stood right before our eyes.

【What a vulgar story.】

The Yoo Joonghyuk of the 1,865th regression. Using [Red Phoenix Shunpo] that had reached its absolute limit, he closed the distance and began unleashing slashes from a range far closer than before.

Odin moved both hands in a flurry and absorbed the slashes into the void dimension.

Yet Yoo Joonghyuk seemed not to care about such minor issues and began swinging his sword at an even greater speed. In the chain of slashes that had become fast enough to distort even space and time, suddenly Yoo Joonghyuk's sword momentum changed, and Odin's expression, previously composed, began to twist little by little.

Shooting Star Slash.

A trajectory of death that no star could evade flew toward Odin's neck. But Odin, as if he had anticipated even that, unleashed a story.

「He stood before endless destruction and never ceased his obsession with infinite knowledge and martial power.」

His single eye that saw the future was reading the trajectory of the Shooting Star Slash.

A strike he barely managed to dodge. But the attack did not end there.

Shooting Star Slash — Two Consecutive Strikes.

The slash that could cut down even Myth-grade Constellations increased to two. Toward the flying slashes, Odin blew a long whistle. Then a flock of crows that covered the sky appeared from somewhere.

Kkaaaaawww!

The crows soon melted like black paint and poured down onto the ground. The black paint erased the entire landscape of the area and even swallowed the Shooting Star Slashes. Within that metaphysical landscape, Odin spoke in his true voice.

[This is my stage! No matter who you are, here you cannot defeat m—]

Shooting Star Slash — Three Consecutive Strikes.

As a slash several times larger than before came crashing down, a look of bewilderment appeared on Odin's face as well.

Perhaps blocking the attacks was gradually becoming overwhelming. He avoided the first slash, barely blocked the second, but the third slash grazed his shoulder. Yoo Joonghyuk did not miss that opening.

Shooting Star Slash — Four Consecutive Strikes.

Odin's single eye trembled violently. He urgently waved both hands and summoned a massive void dimension.

As the void dimension that absorbed even starlight swirled like a black hole, every single one of Yoo Joonghyuk's furiously flying slashes vanished beyond 'Ginnungagap'. Since Ginnungagap was a 'Dimension Before Records', any slash that entered it inevitably lost its power and was dismantled.

Yet even knowing that fact, Yoo Joonghyuk did not stop attacking. And something unbelievable occurred.

Shooting Star Slash — Five Consecutive Strikes.

The moment the five trajectories were sucked into the void dimension, a crack appeared for the first time at the entrance of 'Ginnungagap'.

[What—]

The void dimension that had absorbed the slashes was suffering.

「There is no tragedy that Ginnungagap cannot digest.」

The history of a regressor who had turned back time again and again. The slash carrying that story was advancing while shattering even the void itself.

Shooting Star Slash — Six Consecutive Strikes.

The collapse of Ginnungagap's entrance and Odin's scream occurred at almost the same time.

[How dare you—]

The slashes continued, deeply cutting his right hand and left hand, his abdomen, both of his legs, and then his chest.

[GHAAAAHHH!]

Odin's incarnation body was being torn to pieces. Even after mercilessly severing Vili dwelling in Odin's right hand and Vé in his left, the Shooting Star Slash did not stop.

Shooting Star Slash — Seven Consecutive Strikes.

As if determined to shred every story of that star to pieces, the Shooting Star Slash carved out even Odin's scream. Odin's empty eyes blinked. The moment the tattered Odin slowly sank to his knees, Yoo Joonghyuk's 'Black Heavenly Demon Sword' pierced through Odin's heart.

「A great myth was collapsing right before their eyes.」

The strange thing, however, was Odin's expression. Even in the moment facing his end, Odin was smiling while gripping the blade of Yoo Joonghyuk's 'Black Heavenly Demon Sword'.

「He is the god who offered nine lives over nine nights while pierced by a spear.」

In that instant, Odin's story flashed through my mind.

「He offered himself as a sacrifice upon the World Tree 'Yggdrasil' and glimpsed the 'First Record'.」

Odin's incarnation body was enveloped in dazzling rays of light. The moment the Yoo Joonghyuk of the 1,865th regression hesitated, the story that flowed from Odin's entire body grew like chains and bound Yoo Joonghyuk's whole body. Frowning, Yoo Joonghyuk tried to shake them off. But he could not cut the story chains.

「The magical chain Gleipnir.」

It was a star relic that Odin possessed. Yet no matter how great a star relic it was, it should not have been able to control Yoo Joonghyuk. Under normal circumstances, that should have been the case.

「There are three ways to survive in a ruined world.」

If only it had not been for the story dwelling in the Disaster.

「I have already forgotten some of them by now.」

It was the story of 'Ways of Survival'.

Odin, resurrected within the rays of light, gripped the chain that bound Yoo Joonghyuk as if holding a leash.

[You foolish Disaster. Did you truly think I would be defeated?]

Yoo Joonghyuk unleashed an even more powerful status to try to release the story chains, but the chains only tightened more strongly around his entire body.

「It was a leash created solely to control a single disaster.」

Odin spoke with a faint smile.

[Give up. This is the leash meant to bind the Wolf of Disaster.]

Within the story of 'Ragnarok', 'Gleipnir' was the star relic that bound the disaster Fenrir.

As long as Yoo Joonghyuk from the 1,865th regression had descended as the 'Wolf That Swallowed God', he could not escape this restraint.

[This is the great 'Ragnarok'. No matter what you do, you cannot undo this lea—]

Accompanied by fierce sparks, a Probability storm began raging from Yoo Joonghyuk's entire body.

【Do you—】

Part of Gleipnir was composed of the story of 'Ways of Survival' that Odin had obtained. And 'Ways of Survival' was the birth story that constituted Yoo Joonghyuk himself. Therefore, it should have been impossible for Yoo Joonghyuk to sever that 'Story' with his own power.

【—think this is the first time I face you?】

That would have been the case only if he had been the Yoo Joonghyuk of 'Ways of Survival'.

Gleipnir's binding, which should never have been broken, began to snap.

「He was the Yoo Joonghyuk of the 1,865th regression. The protagonist of the story who had gone beyond 'Ways of Survival' and finally walked out of that stage.」

Odin's eyes began to widen as he confirmed the story flowing throughout Yoo Joonghyuk's entire body.

The Myth-grade Constellation who had witnessed a story he had never seen before shook his head and stepped backward instinctively.

[The Story 'Terror of Asgard' has begun its storytelling.]

It was the exclusive story of Yoo Joonghyuk who cleared the scenarios of the 1,865th regression.

[How can you possess that story—]

Throwing the powerless, broken chains onto the ground, Yoo Joonghyuk of the 1,865th regression slowly rose to his feet.

【This is not the first time I'm going to kill you.】

The moment the 'Wolf That Swallowed God' unleashed his status, Odin also prepared for the final battle.

As the statuses of the two beings collided, a pitch black night descended upon the area.

「This was an event whose ending was already predetermined.」

Suffocating silence covered the battlefield. The 'Nameless Ones' that had been lingering nearby, drawn by Yoo Joonghyuk's story, could not endure the story pressure of the two existences that suddenly crashed down and exploded. Within that horrific feast, only I and the Yoo Joonghyuk of the 41st regression foresaw the conclusion of this story.

"We have to help Odin."

— No.

Without taking my eyes off Odin and the Yoo Joonghyuk of the 1,865th regression, I spoke.

— You know it too, don't you? How this battle ends.

Yoo Joonghyuk of the 41st regression did not answer. He also knew.

— This is the only way we can win.

A story was boiling within the Yoo Joonghyuk of the 41st regression. It was the story of 'Vidar, the Ender of Wars', whom he had once killed.

「The myth of 'Ragnarok' was being reenacted.」

Within the pitch black night sky, Odin's beloved steed 'Sleipnir' could be seen galloping across space. Odin mounted his steed and gripped his exclusive weapon. A spear that spat flames of light (光炎).

The Divine Spear Gungnir.

Containing the story of <Asgard>, it was the spear that had tormented Yoo Joonghyuk and <Kim Dokja Company> again and again in the past.

Facing that spear, Yoo Joonghyuk also drew his Black Heavenly Demon Sword.

If he had been the Yoo Joonghyuk of the 1,865th regression, it would have been difficult to overwhelm Odin with pure martial power alone. But the current him was different.

Right now he was an incarnation who had seen the conclusion twice, a regressor who had reached the final point of regression. And—

Breaking the Sky Swordsmanship (破天劍道).

Secret Technique: Inner Mystery Transmission (超越奧義).

—the disaster that swallows stars.

Galaxy Slash (銀河斬).

Before the blade that cut down an entire myth, the strongest constellation who had ruled the primordial void smiled.

[With this—]

The final chapter of the grand 'Ragnarok' was at last raising its curtain.

[Asgard is eternal.]

Yoo Joonghyuk of the 1,865th regression exhaled roughly and withdrew his story.

His vision was hazy. It was probably because he had exerted excessive power.

【Kim Dokja.】

A name he called without thinking. The sound of his own voice calling that name felt unfamiliar.

At some point it had come to resemble the voice of an Outer God.

His ominous face was reflected on the blade of the Black Heavenly Demon Sword stained with story.

「The story of the dead Odin was scattering across the world.」

The fragments of Odin that scattered like pure white starlight soon became part of the 'Stage'. Upon that stage stood another Yoo Joonghyuk blocking his path.

[A Giant Story is being realized!]

Odin had probably already foreseen his own death.

「This was the only method that could kill the 'Wolf That Swallowed God'.」

As the stage of 'Ragnarok' strengthened, an astonishing story power began surging throughout the entire body of Yoo Joonghyuk from the 41st regression.

「He was the great star that hunted the final wolf.」

Through his own death, Odin had passed the [Fate] of 'Ragnarok' to the Yoo Joonghyuk of the 41st regression.

Yoo Joonghyuk of the 1,865th regression watched that sight with indifferent eyes and asked.

【Is the thing you trusted merely the power of that 'stage'?】

As if looking down upon the stage of 'Ragnarok', the Yoo Joonghyuk of the 1,865th regression raised his status.

[The Giant Story 'Ragnarok' is swaying dangerously!]

Certainly the Giant Story of 'Ragnarok' was powerful. But he was an existence who had already seen the ending twice. No story of this world could completely bind him to a 'Stage'. Yet at that moment the Yoo Joonghyuk of the 41st regression opened his mouth.

"You—"

The moment he heard that voice, he realized it was not the voice of the '41st regression'.

「Kim Dokja.」

Another Kim Dokja dwelling within the Yoo Joonghyuk of the 41st regression was asking him.

"Does this place still look like 'Ragnarok' to you?"

Clad in Odin's starlight, the coat that Yoo Joonghyuk of the 41st regression was wearing shone pure white and took on a white hue.

For a moment, Yoo Joonghyuk from the 1,865th regression looked down at his own pitch black coat.

「Only then did Yoo Joonghyuk realize the meaning of this battlefield.」

This was a confrontation between Yoo Joonghyuk and Yoo Joonghyuk.

Two Yoo Joonghyuks wearing coats of different colors faced each other.

One on the ground, and the other in the sky. The moment he realized that he himself was within that landscape, Yoo Joonghyuk of the 1,865th regression recalled a very old story.

「Once, he had stood in their place.」

In the days when he firmly believed himself to be the Yoo Joonghyuk of the 3rd regression. He had stood there together with Kim Dokja. Standing there, he had faced a certain Outer God that seemed absolutely impossible to defeat.

'So this is how you felt, you bastard.'

In the sky, where that Outer God had once stood, he now stood alone.

[The Great Disaster 'Wolf That Swallowed God' is roaring.]

Perhaps it was only at that moment that Yoo Joonghyuk understood another version of himself from a very long time ago. The moment understanding was established, accompanied by the sound of an old page turning, the landscape of the surrounding area began to ripple.

[The 'Stage' is resonating.]

The distant sound of water from the Tongtian River covered the battlefield of 'Ragnarok'.

[A new Story has been born.]

The two Yoo Joonghyuks pointed spear and sword at each other, and at last an old story began to be reenacted.

[The Story 'Qualification of the Protagonist' has begun its storytelling.]
