<title>1065 Episode 67. The World Serpent (3)
Jung Heewon stared blankly at the man's side profile.
"You are—"
Having possessed the flesh of the 1,864th round in the world of the 'Third Kim Dokja', Jung Heewon had met him before.
<&>「His name was 'Lee Hyunsung'. The 'Lee Hyunsung of the 1,864th round'.」
There were things she remembered vividly. Things like the somewhat burdensome goodwill and the warm gaze. But the emotion felt from him right now was not the warmth of back then.
<&>「The man standing by her side right now stood here solely to protect a single person.」
From a person who has built an upright history, their sincerity is vividly felt even without words. This man also belonged to that kind.
"I roughly heard the story from Dokja-ssi."
"Dokja-ssi?"
Only upon seeing Lee Hyunsung nodding faintly did Jung Heewon realize that he had come here under Kim Dokja's secret order. Kim Dokja probably knew things would turn out like this.
"Why did he send you? You're not very strong."
"Pardon?"
"I saw everything back then. You passed out limp after using just a bit of strength."
Jung Heewon had already seen Lee Hyunsung fight in the world of the 'Third Kim Dokja'. In truth, he had been beaten up by ordinary men in suits there and lost consciousness.
"That was... that world was a place where I couldn't exert my strength."
Lee Hyunsung abruptly coughed dryly and pointed at his forearm. His forearm, glowing silver, certainly looked thicker than back then. Glaring intently at those muscles, Jung Heewon spoke.
"Aren't those balloon muscles?"
"Pardon? No, they aren't! From now on, I will prove—"
"In this world, such bulky muscles aren't needed. Lean muscles are far more advantageous. It's also more convenient to utilize stories or skills. And simple differences in strength can be resolved in many other ways."
Every time Jung Heewon said a word, Lee Hyunsung's neck retracted like a turtle's.
"Everything Heewon-ssi said is correct, but even so—"
"I'm grateful you came to help, but just stay back. You'll only get in the way."
"No, wait..."
The moment the flustered Lee Hyunsung tried to stop Jung Heewon as she stepped forward, a distant true voice resonated from the head of the 'World Serpent'.
<#>【Hyunsungie hyung?】
Jung Heewon and Lee Hyunsung raised their heads at the same time.
<#>【It's Hyunsungie hyung, right!】
<#>【Hyunsung ahjussi!】
Voices that seemed to hold the emotion called yearning deep in the mouth, rolling it on the tip of the tongue over and over again. Just by hearing those voices, Jung Heewon could realize what kind of meaning this man held to their owners.
Lee Hyunsung drew in a deep breath along with a low sigh, then slowly blinked his eyes. Accompanied by a pitch-black glint in his eyes, a sound like iron forged in deep darkness came from his voice.
<#>【Yes, it is me.】
Startled, Jung Heewon looked back at him. The man's expression, which had felt purely naive until just now, had vanished somewhere, replaced by the face of an Outer God who had weathered a long tragedy.
<#>【Please step back. I will handle those children.】
The overwhelming status radiating from his entire body proved the years he had lived and his martial prowess. Even so, Jung Heewon did not back down.
"This isn't a stage for you to step forward."
Rather, that appearance of his, and the joy dwelling in those voices, stirred opposition within her.
<#>【No, this is a stage I must take responsibility for.】
"What are you?"
<#>【The person responsible for all these tragedies.】
The emotion contained in that grave voice was undoubtedly true. But truth could not always persuade everyone.
"Do you think you're any different from that disaster?"
At Jung Heewon's sharp voice, Lee Hyunsung looked back at her once more.
"What do you know about this world's stories?"
Jung Heewon knew she shouldn't say that. She too was feeling the poignant stories emanating from the man before her eyes. She could also gauge to some degree how terrible a life he had endured to reach this place.
"Without even knowing what kind of history I lived here, or what kind of life I lived."
Yet even so, she could not help but speak like that. Perhaps it was because she was frustrated. Because in the most important scenario, facing an insurmountable obstacle that could not possibly be overcome with her power made her so immensely frustrated.
"Without even knowing properly what happened, doing whatever you want—"
<#>【Yes, I do not know.】
Lee Hyunsung nodded obediently.
<#>【Because I do not know, I came here wanting to understand even a little.】
He advanced forward like a great mountain. With momentum that seemed unyielding to anything, he stood right before the World Serpent.
<#>【Step down, children. I do not wish to fight.】
Before his ringing true voice, the disaster fell silent for a moment. A feeling as if the air was churning heavily. Soon, an answer returned from the top of the World Serpent.
<#>【Why are you trying to stop us?】
A boy's voice that sounded somewhat wronged.
<#>【Hyung knows too, doesn't he? This worldline was originally fated to perish. This place is—】
The 41st round was a world whose outcome was already determined. Words she had heard several times from various people already. The moment Jung Heewon bit her lower lip hard and was about to say something.
<#>【That is not important.】
With a somewhat surprised expression, Jung Heewon looked at Lee Hyunsung.
<#>【Dokja-ssi asked me, so I merely follow his wish. Therefore, this time, you two must give up.】
At his calm warning, the body of the 'World Serpent' trembled faintly. A vibration containing deep sorrow. As if measuring Lee Hyunsung's sincerity, the disaster silently gazed down at him from a dizzying height, then asked back.
<#>【And if we don't give up?】
Lee Hyunsung silently raised both hands. Jung Heewon was purely astonished by that action. He had merely raised his hands, yet there was a force as if an entire mountain range were moving.
<#>【Ah...】
Did she realize the man was serious? The girl groaned as if sighing. Subsequently, the boy sharply asked back.
<#>【Aren't you underestimating us too much?】
The colossal body of the World Serpent vibrated once more. Beyond that faint tremor, the boy's agitated voice was heard.
<#>【No matter how strong hyung is, do you think you can stop us alone?】
At first, she thought the color of the sky had reversed. The pitch-black sky turning deep bluish-green. But looking closely, it was not merely the sky being colored.
<&>「The sky was melting entirely.」
Would it look like that if a story of poison were condensed to its limit? The pitch-black night sky was melting and flowing down from the poison. Soon, the speed at which the sky melted gradually accelerated. Jung Heewon instinctively perceived it. That poison could not be withstood.
A wave of poison on a different dimension from the poisonous fog Yamata no Orochi had used poured over their heads, melting the world. In an instant, all sorts of simulations ran through her mind.
<&>「What if I use Intent-Formed Sword?」
<&>「What if I counter with Breaking the Sky Meteor Strike?」
<&>「What if I cleave the sky once more with [Single Sword Cleaving the Sky]?」
Yet no matter what she did, it didn't seem possible to cut through that tidal wave of poison surging down after melting the entire firmament.
<#>【Lee Gilyoung! Don't!】
The girl's breathless cry. But the tidal wave of poison had already surged to several tens of meters above. Finally, the moment the resolved Jung Heewon gripped her sword hilt, Lee Hyunsung quietly held her back with his right hand. And without any change in expression, he serenely spread his left palm toward the firmament.
<&>「Wall of Steel.」
A translucent curtain of steel manifested around him and her. Jung Heewon was flustered. Normally, she would have shouted that this was not an attack that could be blocked by such a barrier. But the moment she confirmed the barrier's durability, Jung Heewon was startled out of her wits.
<&>「Jung Heewon had never seen a story metal of such immense hardness.」
Subsequently, the tidal wave of poison poured over their heads. Yet even the poison that melted the sky could not melt the man's steel wall. A story that inspired overwhelming awe, making one feel as if nothing in this world could break the protective wall created by this man.
Just how much history did this man endure to obtain this story?
<&>「"Excuse me for a moment."」
A wall where faint warmth lingered. What she could read was merely the remnants of the story dimly left on this wall. Standing tall atop those remnants, the man answered toward the firmament.
<#>【It seems it is not me, but you who are underestimating.】
As the movement of the poison pouring from midair grew sluggish, Lee Hyunsung dispelled the barrier while simultaneously clapping his hands loudly. A massive gale was generated by the clap, completely scattering all the remaining poisonous fog in the area. Then, a voice was heard from above once again.
<#>【We went easy on you this time! No matter if you're hyung, if you interfere any further—】
Instead of listening to those words, Lee Hyunsung took a stride forward and spoke.
<#>【Stand down.】
With that, both of Lee Hyunsung's hands made contact with the outer hide of the World Serpent. In ordinary circumstances, just because both hands touched it didn't mean anything could be done. For the 'World Serpent' was large enough to wrap around an entire planet. But the one who grasped that serpent in his hands this time was none other than 'Lee Hyunsung'. He was the Steel Sword Emperor, the incarnation of the 'Master of Steel'. The sturdiest shield of <Kim Dokja Company>. And this was the first 'stigma' he had ever learned—
<&>「Great Mountain Push.」
Noticing Lee Hyunsung's intention, the boy shouted from midair.
<#>【No matter how strong hyung is, with that technique—】
The boy's voice vanished along with the vibration that spread immediately after.
<&>「The entire world was trembling.」
[Great Mountain Push]. Initially a stigma that had only amounted to prying open locked subway doors.
<&>「Across the 1,864th round and the 1,865th round, Lee Hyunsung had relentlessly trained this stigma.」
What did it mean to 'push'? Pushing and pushing again, Lee Hyunsung thought about what 'pushing' was.
<&>「To push is, at the same time, to be pushed by it.」
As he firmly planted both legs into the ground, his stories churned.
<&>「To push is to invade another existence's space, and through the felt resistance, to confirm that existence once more.」
Along with Lee Hyunsung's battle cry, the earth, the sky, and the surrounding universe were shaking violently.
<&>「And so, 'to push' is.」
As if pushing the entire 'frame' of the space-time to which a matter belonged, the universe was being pushed away by Lee Hyunsung's strength.
<&>「The act of completely colliding the history one has lived against the opponent.」
The World Serpent that wrapped around the world of the scenario writhed in agony.
Kugugugugu.
The history a man had collided with was pushing back a single ruin.
<#>【If you do not give up.】
Lee Hyunsung's body, which had gradually grown in volume, looked as if he had lifted the 'World Serpent' in its entirety. He added with a true voice carrying terrifying ambition.
<#>【I could throw you to the edge of the universe just like this.】
No one could deny that those words were the truth. That was how strong Lee Hyunsung was, and he possessed the power to realize his story.
It was then that a clicking sound was heard from somewhere.
<#>【Load.】
A voice carrying a bone-chilling coolness.
<#>【Fire.】
Subsequently, along with an explosion that blanketed the earth, a volley of fire poured toward Lee Hyunsung and Jung Heewon.
<&>「Since when had it been there? A single battleship was floating in that place.」
The pale dust cleared, revealing the steel barrier Lee Hyunsung had created. Even though it was a light warning shot, a faint hairline crack had formed on the exterior of the barrier. It meant the existence that had poured out the attack just now was an Outer God possessing an equally powerful status.
<#>【I knew this would happen.】
A voice carrying a sense of déjà vu. Jung Heewon felt her hands trembling. That 'World Serpent', and the Lee Hyunsung beside her. At her current level, they were opponents too daunting to handle. Yet another monster on that level had appeared before her eyes.
<#>【I told you, didn't I? That in the end, it might turn out like this.】
Taking the melted sky as an ocean, a colossal battleship was floating as if suspended. Atop that battleship was a woman radiating an immense status. A ponytail fluttering pitch-black in the wind. Jung Heewon's eyes widened.
<#>【Jihye-ya. Stand down. This time, even I cannot concede.】
When Lee Hyunsung warned first, Lee Jihye frowned.
<#>【Hyunsung ahjussi. We have a conclusion we want.】
Spitting out the bubble gum she had been chewing, Lee Jihye spoke.
<#>【And ahjussi has no right to stop us. Because—】
It was an ancient resentment. As if unraveling the end of long-harbored emotions, Lee Jihye, who had now grown from a girl into a warrior, grasped her Twin Dragon Swords.
<#>【Because at the end of that story, you chose a 'conclusion' different from ours.】
