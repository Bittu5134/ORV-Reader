"Heewon-ssi, is there something you want to do?"

"Something I want to do."

Only upon reaching the final scenario, on the tiny handful of blank space we had barely reached, were we asking each other about what we 'wanted to do'.

"What I want to do is something that can't come true."

"How do you know without trying?"

"Should I just try it?"

Staring at me blankly, Jung Heewon giggled softly and added:

"What I wish for is something impossible now."

Curious about what it was, I asked several more times, but in the end, Jung Heewon wouldn't tell me what she wanted to do. I spoke in a slightly exaggerated tone:

"That's cold."

"Who's the one really being cold to people here?"

"When was I ever cold?"

"You're not really asking because you don't know, are you?"

Looking slightly angry, Jung Heewon held the liquor bottle and tipped all the remaining drink down her throat.

"Heewon-ssi."

"What."

In truth, I knew. What she was upset with me about. But I am that kind of person. And Kim Dokja was that kind of human.

"We will win in the end, and the scenario will be resolved well. I can do it well. Don't worry too much."

At my words, Jung Heewon glared at me with narrowed eyes and said:

"Aren't you thinking that you have to do it, rather than that you can do it?"

"Yes. I have to."

Tapping the empty liquor bottle as if dissatisfied with my answer, Jung Heewon said:

"You're going to use an absurd method again, aren't you?"

"There might be times when I have to."

"Even if I tell you not to, you'll put your life on the line again."

I hesitated for a moment before nodding. Because I felt I had to be honest with her.

Jung Heewon bit her lip tightly.

"Can't we really just run away? What meaning is there in protecting this world to that extent? What even is this world?"

"I am responsible for this story."

I didn't think she would be able to understand this answer. However, everyone has a job they must accomplish, and for me, that was the conclusion of this story.

<!>[The influence of the Fable, 'Successor of the Eternal Name', grows stronger.]

Why was that? At that moment, it felt as if Jung Heewon was looking into a story inside me that I couldn't even look into myself.
