<#>【Jihye-ya.】
Lee Hyunsung looked up at Lee Jihye with a hardened expression. In the eyes of the girl who had now become an adult, a cold resolve dwelled.
<#>【I did not come to fight.】
<#>【You came to stop us anyway. Then you should've been prepared to fight.】
Lee Jihye finished speaking with a frigid expression and promptly extended her right hand. At that, the cannons of her battleship reminiscent of a turtle ship—Turtle Dragon—aimed at the ground all at once.
<#>【I'm not joking, ahjussi. If you don't back down now, I won't go easy on you.】
<#>【Jihye-ya, I—】
<#>【Load.】

<#>【Heewon unnie?】
Jung Heewon, who had been awkwardly gripping her sword hilt beside Lee Hyunsung, also looked at her. The moment the two gazes met, memories rose.
<&>「"Unnie!"」
Jung Heewon remembered Lee Jihye. Because in this world, she had fought alongside Lee Jihye several times. Yet the memories that surfaced were not just one.
<&>「"Uh...... I'm a bit weak to cool unnies."」
Along with sparks of tzztzztzz, memories from a time she had never lived rose in her mind. Jung Heewon realized that this was a memory of the 1,864th round. A faintly welling dizziness and an emotion of yearning that densely filled her heart. Even though they were not her own emotions, Jung Heewon stumbled for a moment, intoxicated by that story.
After gazing at Jung Heewon for a moment, Lee Jihye slowly turned her head and asked.
<#>【Doesn't seem to be the unnie I know. Where is Heewon unnie?】
Just by looking past Jung Heewon, Lee Jihye seemed to have noticed that she was not the Jung Heewon she remembered.
<#>【Unnie chose that conclusion together with ahjussi, didn't she? So why is ahjussi alone? Where's unnie?】
<#>【Heewon-ssi is......】

<&>「He knew that this was not Lee Jihye's true feelings.」
Though Lee Jihye was sometimes aggressive, she was not someone who would say such words to her companions. If she gave such a retort, there was only one reason.
<#>【Go back even now. It is still not too late.】
No one here wanted to point swords at each other. They were once companions, and people who wanted to see the end of the same story. Even though the conclusion they chose was different, perhaps even that conclusion ultimately originated from their feelings toward a single person.

<&>「Giant of the Sea, Aegir.」
As her Twin Dragon Swords pointed to the ground, an ocean began to churn above the melted sky.
<#>【Final warning. If you don't go back—】
The next moment, Jung Heewon's figure vanished. [Red Phoenix Shunpo] narrowing the distance in an instant. Startled, Lee Jihye countered with [Ghost Walk].
Accompanied by a sharp screeching friction, the two swords let out a violent cry. A strong story pressure felt in her grip. Lee Jihye opened her mouth in a voice mixed with pure admiration.
<#>【What's this? How did you train to this extent in a mere 41st round?】
"A mere 41st round. I really want to stop hearing that."
<#>【Why? I only said the truth.】
Instead of answering, Jung Heewon swung her sword. Watching that sword trajectory, Lee Jihye activated a skill as if intrigued.
[Ghost Slayer].
Brilliant ghostly flames bloomed in Lee Jihye's pupils. Jung Heewon countered with the same [Ghost Slayer]. If Lee Jihye's [Ghost Slayer] was a skill trained to its limit, Jung Heewon's [Ghost Slayer] was slightly different. Even if its proficiency fell short, it was because she had the attribute effect of 'Butcher of Madness'.
Kagagagak!
Receiving the synergy of the attribute effect, Jung Heewon's physical capabilities were on par with Lee Jihye's for a moment. After pure sword strikes collided several times, the first to step back was Lee Jihye.
<#>【You wield a sword this well? In a mere 41st round?】
Subsequently, Lee Jihye's Twin Dragon Swords changed their position. Lee Hyunsung, as well as Shin Yoosung and Lee Gilyoung watching the fierce battle from the firmament, recognized that technique of Lee Jihye's.
<&>「Instant Kill (瞬殺)」
Lee Jihye's fatal technique that sent the heads of even the stars in that sky flying in a single strike.
<#>【Jihye-ya! No!】
When Lee Hyunsung's roar echoed, her Twin Dragon Swords had already approached Jung Heewon's throat. A strike that could not be handled with reflexes. Almost instinctively, Jung Heewon swung her sword.
<&>「Formless Sword Qi.」
Dozens of strands of Formless Sword Qi unleashed from her entire body subtly bent the sword tip of Instant Kill. Subsequently, her reflexes, reacting a beat late, barely dragged her body out from the domain of Instant Kill.
Spat.
Seeing Jung Heewon dodge the attack by a split-second interval, a smile bloomed on Lee Jihye's lips.
<#>【You dodged it? Then how about this?】
A chain of strikes of [Instant Kill] unfolded. A barrage of blows that could not be dodged by mere luck alone. Gritting her teeth, Jung Heewon unleashed Formless Sword Qi to deflect the trajectory of [Instant Kill] somehow. Realizing the situation was turning urgent, as Lee Hyunsung ran over, Lee Jihye spread her left hand.
<#>【Fire.】
At the bombardment pouring from the battleship, Lee Hyunsung could not dare approach, shouting Lee Jihye's name.
<&>「This was the power of the 'Monarch of the Great Sea' who had seen the conclusion twice.」
The martial power to pin down Lee Hyunsung with bombardment and overwhelm the 41st round's Jung Heewon, who could compete with a Myth-grade Constellation.
<#>【Not bad, 41st round's Heewon unnie.】
Yet the 41st round's Jung Heewon did not collapse easily either. She had a reason why she must not collapse. As if already knowing that reason, Lee Jihye changed her stance once more.

Pouring bombardment. In the distance, Lee Hyunsung, having used Steel Transformation, was rushing over, but the bombardment had already arrived right before Jung Heewon's eyes.
Breaking the Sky Swordsmanship. Secret Absolute Technique. Single Sword Cleaving the Sky.
Heavy sword gang cleaved the sky. Bombardment exploded like fireworks in the firmament. Yet even with the Secret Absolute Technique containing all her power, she could not block that bombardment. The sound of blood splashing and bones breaking. In an instant, her vision wavered, and one of Jung Heewon's knees buckled under the bombardment.

"What is so proud about experiencing the scenarios twice?"

"So what did you gain after seeing the end twice?"
As another round of bombardment passed, Jung Heewon noticed that sensation had vanished from her left shoulder.
<#>【Fire.】

"All you're doing is coming all the way here to destroy someone else's worldline."
Was it because of the resentment dwelling in that vividness? In a somewhat agitated voice, Lee Jihye pushed back.
<#>【You cannot speak so carelessly. We—】
Jung Heewon's other knee, which had been barely enduring, buckled as well.
<#>【We merely—】

<#>【Unnie! No!】

But some of the bombardment had already reached Jung Heewon's proximity. Arriving at a razor-thin timing, Lee Hyunsung deployed Steel Transformation to take the brunt of the shelling.
Kugugugugu!

It was not something Lee Hyunsung or Shin Yoosung had done. Shells flying from somewhere. Someone was pouring artillery fire toward Turtle Dragon.
<#>【What bastard—】

<&>「The Ghost Fleet of the 41st round.」

"Uh?"

Receiving that still-innocent gaze, the 1,864th round's Lee Jihye—the 'Monarch of the Great Sea'—tightly shut her eyes.

<&>「Outer God (外神).」
The term referring to evil gods existing outside the scenarios, plundering the stories of those who live the scenarios. Only then did Lee Jihye realize what she had become.
<#>【Jihye-ya.】
Lee Hyunsung, who had raised his body from amidst the shelling, spoke.
<#>【Our story already ended long ago.】
She had known that kind of thing from the start. And it was not words she wanted to hear from Lee Hyunsung's mouth.
<#>【I know that too.】

That story written by Han Sooyoung, lived by Yoo Joonghyuk, and read by Kim Dokja—she too had read it as she came here.
